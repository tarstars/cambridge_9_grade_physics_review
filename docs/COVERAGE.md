## The genuine gaps to source (forward-looking)

These are mostly **IGCSE content he'll meet *during* grade 10**, not "previous-class" foundations —
so lower priority for the immediate gap-diagnosis, but needed for full grade-10 coverage:

1. **4.5 Electromagnetic effects** — induction, motors, generators, transformers.
   - Free: **Siyavula Physical Sciences Gr 11**, or **NCERT Class 10 "Magnetic Effects of Electric Current"**.
2. **5.2 Radioactivity** — alpha/beta/gamma, half-life, safety.
   - Free options are weaker at this level; best is an IGCSE coursebook. CK-12 physics has a unit.
3. **6 Space physics** — Solar System, orbits, life cycle of stars, redshift / Big Bang.
   - Free: CK-12 astronomy/earth-science; BBC Bitesize (revision, not a PDF). Cleanest is an IGCSE coursebook.

> The single commercial resource that covers gaps 2 and 3 cleanly is the **Cambridge IGCSE
> Physics Coursebook** (0625-aligned). Same situation as the textbook note in `textbook/SOURCES.md`.

## Bottom line

- **Foundations + IGCSE core (topics 1–4): strong** — well covered for the "previous-class gaps" the teacher flagged.
- **Forward IGCSE topics (4.5, 5.2, 6): gaps** — fine for now; source as he reaches them.
