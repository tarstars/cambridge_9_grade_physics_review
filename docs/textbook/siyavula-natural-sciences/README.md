## Why this is the "Grade 9" answer

In the South African curriculum, **"Physical Sciences" only starts at Grade 10** — which is
why the other Siyavula book in `../siyavula-physical-sciences/` is Grade 10. For Grades 7–9
the subject is the integrated **"Natural Sciences,"** so *this* is the genuine grade-9-level
Siyavula book, matching the age/level of Cambridge Lower Secondary Stage 9.

## Physics content (it's an integrated science book, so also has biology/earth/space)

Grade-9-level coverage of:
- **Forces & friction**, gravity
- **Work, energy & simple machines**, energy conversion
- **Electricity** — including consumption and the cost of electrical energy
- **Heat** / thermal energy
- **Sound**; some **magnetism / electromagnets**

## Honest limitations vs the Cambridge syllabus

This book is pitched right by level, but it is **lighter** on a few specific Cambridge topics:
- Detailed **circuit analysis** (series/parallel current, resistance = V/I) — thin here.
- **Geometric optics** (reflection, refraction, dispersion, colour) — barely covered.
- Thermal **transfer mechanisms** (conduction/convection/radiation) — not deep.

For those, the **Grade 10 Physical Sciences** book (`../siyavula-physical-sciences/`) and the
**CK-12** worksheets (`../ck12-physical-science/`) go further. So: use this for grade-9-level
**forces / energy / general electricity / heat / sound**, and the Grade 10 + CK-12 for the
deeper electricity, optics and thermal-transfer detail.
