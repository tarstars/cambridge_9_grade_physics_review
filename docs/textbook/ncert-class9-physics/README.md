# NCERT Class 9 Science — complete book (free, legal full text)

The **full** Class 9 Science book, downloaded from the official NCERT site
(https://ncert.nic.in): **2026 edition**, *"Exploration: Entering the World of Secondary
Science"* — 13 chapters + prelims. Indian curriculum (not Cambridge), openly published and
free to download.

**Layout — physics vs. everything else:**
- **Top level** = the 4 **physics** chapters (this project's focus).
- **`other-chapters/`** = the rest of the book (intro, biology, chemistry, earth, and the
  book's front matter/Contents).

## Physics coverage (this edition)

Covers **motion** (ch4), **forces / Newton's laws / momentum** (ch6), **work, energy, power &
simple machines** (ch7), **sound & waves** (ch10). No standalone chapter for **light/optics,
electricity & magnetism, heat, or pressure** — use CK‑12, Siyavula, or the Cambridge Stage
books (see `../SOURCES.md` and `review/study-references.md`).
