# CK-12 — Physical Science for Middle School (free)

Free resources from the CK-12 Foundation (https://www.ck12.org), downloaded from CK-12's own
CDN. Pitched at **middle school**, which is a good level for a grade-9 review and for
**practice / self-study tasks**.

## Files here

| File | Pages | Use |
|---|:---:|---|
| `CK-12-Physical-Science-MS-Workbook-with-Answers.pdf` | 436 | Six worksheets per lesson (multiple choice, T/F, vocabulary, reading/writing) — **with answers**. Great source of weekly self-study tasks. |
| `CK-12-Physical-Science-MS-Quizzes-and-Tests-with-Answers.pdf` | 359 | Ready-made quizzes & tests **with answers** — useful for the Week-0 diagnostic and Week-8 re-test. |

Both cover the gap topics — **electricity, magnetism, light/optics (incl. reflection &
refraction), and heat/energy** — as well as motion, forces and sound.

## The full FlexBook (read online)

The complete CK-12 Physical Science FlexBook (the explanatory text, not just worksheets) is
free to read online but needs a free CK-12 account to export as PDF:
- https://flexbooks.ck12.org/cbook/ck-12-middle-school-physical-science-flexbook-2.0/

Use the Siyavula book (`../siyavula-physical-sciences/`) or NCERT for offline reading text;
use these CK-12 PDFs for questions and practice.
