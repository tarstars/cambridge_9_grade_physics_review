# Textbook — Cambridge Lower Secondary Science 9 (2nd edition, 2021)

Authors: Mary Jones, Diane Fellowes-Freeman, Michael Smyth · Publisher: Cambridge University Press & Assessment

Physics is one strand of this combined-science series. The two books most useful for your plan:

- **Learner's Book 9** — the textbook (explanations to read). ISBN **9781108742863**.
- **Workbook 9** — Focus / Practice / Challenge exercises. ISBN **9781108742894**.
  Good source of the **weekly self-study tasks** you wanted to set.

> **Target is now grade-10 IGCSE Physics 0625** (foundation = this Lower Secondary set). See
> `docs/COVERAGE.md` for how the downloaded books map to the six 0625 topics and where the
> gaps are. The cleanest single resource for the IGCSE-specific gaps (radioactivity, space
> physics) is the **Cambridge IGCSE Physics Coursebook** (0625-aligned) — same buy-vs-free
> situation as below.

## 3. Free, fully-legal full-text fallback (different curriculum) — DOWNLOADED

**NCERT Class 9 Science** (Indian government curriculum) is openly and legally published. Not
Cambridge, but clearly written and free. I've downloaded the **physics chapters** of the
current **2026 edition** into:

```
docs/textbook/ncert-class9-physics/   (see its README.md for the chapter→topic map)
  ch04-describing-motion.pdf
  ch06-how-forces-affect-motion.pdf
  ch07-work-energy-simple-machines.pdf
  ch10-sound-waves.pdf
```

**Heads-up — partial coverage.** The 2026 NCERT Class 9 book has no standalone chapter for
**Light, Electricity & magnetism, or Heat**, so it covers only ~half the checklist. Use the
Cambridge Learner's Book for those. Full details in `ncert-class9-physics/README.md`.

- Source (to browse other chapters): https://ncert.nic.in/textbook.php  (Class 9 → Science)

## 4. More free, legal full-text — fills the NCERT gaps — DOWNLOADED

NCERT alone covers ~half the checklist. These openly-licensed / free books cover the rest
(Light, Electricity & magnetism, Heat) and are downloaded:

```
docs/textbook/siyavula-natural-sciences/    Siyavula Natural Sciences Gr 9 (204 pp, CC-BY) — grade-9 LEVEL
docs/textbook/siyavula-physical-sciences/   Siyavula Physical Sciences Gr 10 (512 pp) — one level up, deeper
docs/textbook/ck12-physical-science/        CK-12 worksheets + quizzes/tests (with answers)
```

- **Siyavula Natural Sciences Gr 9** (CC-BY): the genuine *grade-9-level* book — forces,
  energy & machines, general electricity, heat, sound. In the SA curriculum "Physical
  Sciences" only starts at Grade 10, so Grade 9 = the integrated "Natural Sciences" subject.
  Lighter on detailed circuits, optics and thermal transfer (use Gr 10 / CK-12 for those).
- **Siyavula Physical Sciences Gr 10** (CC-licensed): one level up but goes deeper — heat,
  electrostatics, electric circuits, magnetism, EM radiation, waves, sound. Source:
  https://www.siyavula.com/read
- **CK-12** (free): practice worksheets + ready-made quizzes/tests covering electricity,
  light/optics, heat. Full FlexBook reads online (free account to export):
  https://flexbooks.ck12.org/cbook/ck-12-middle-school-physical-science-flexbook-2.0/

See each folder's `README.md` for the topic→file map. Together with NCERT, these give a
**complete free, legal set** keyed to the diagnostic.

### Recommendation

You now have a **complete free, legal set** already downloaded: **NCERT** (motion, forces,
energy, sound) + **Siyavula** (heat, electricity, magnetism, waves) + **CK-12** (practice
worksheets, quizzes/tests). That covers the whole checklist at no cost.

If you'd rather use the actual Cambridge book, the legit route is the **Learner's Book 9 +
Workbook 9** digital-access edition (the Workbook makes the weekly self-study loop easy);
confirm fit first via the free Issuu previews. The diagnostic in `review/checklist.md` works
with any of these — it's keyed to the official syllabus, not to a specific book.
