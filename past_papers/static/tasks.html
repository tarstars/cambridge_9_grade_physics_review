Each task is one numbered question. Structured subparts, such as 2(a) and 2(b), belong to the same task. Open a question to see its original PDF content, including diagrams and continuation pages.
Physics topics are suggested from that question’s content. You can change its main topic, add other topics, or confirm the suggestions. “Topics needing review” finds weak or missing suggestions. Topic corrections survive re-indexing.
Each question has its own To process, Know how to solve, or Solved mark and notes. A group mark is separate. “Mark all … questions” applies to all questions in that filtered group, across every results page.
Existing document marks are kept in Full papers. They do not automatically mark individual questions. Export and restore include question marks, topic corrections, group marks, and existing paper progress.
Question previews are rendered locally from the downloaded PDFs. You can also open the source PDF at the exact page and use its matching mark scheme.
