Your library, your pace.

Browse full exam papers by year, session, or paper type. Use Topical practice to focus on a topic, and Worked solutions for source-provided answers.

  • To process — add a paper to your practice queue.
  • Know how to solve — you understand the approach.
  • Solved — you have completed the paper.

Each group has its own mark and notes. A group mark leaves individual paper marks as they are. Use Mark all … papers or select checkboxes to change multiple paper marks.

Downloaded PDFs open locally. A link labelled online opens the original file at PapaCambridge. Reference files include examiner reports, grade thresholds, and practical instructions.

Progress is saved by the Python backend in SQLite. Use Export progress for a portable backup; Restore merges a backup into your current progress, replacing marks for matching papers and groups.
