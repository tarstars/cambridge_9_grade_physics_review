# 8‑week plan · Strategy A — quick‑wins first

> Alternative: [`plan-by-topic.md`](plan-by-topic.md) clears the 0s by large topic instead.

Built from the baseline (`results.json`, 53/160). Runs ~early July → end of August, one **2‑hour
discussion** per week plus **self‑study during the week**. "Read" locations are in
[`study-references.md`](study-references.md); the `gX‑NN` codes are the test questions in the
web tool (`assessment_tool/`).

**How each week works** (your model):
1. **His week — self‑study:** he reads the listed sections and does the practice.
2. **Your 2 h — discuss & test:** he explains it back; you work a couple of the tool's *Practice
   problems* together; then **re‑score those questions in the tool** (watch 0→1 and 1→2).
3. Anything still shaky rolls into the next week.

Order logic: quick wins first (morale), then the Grade‑8 gaps he *should* already have, then the
Grade‑9 material (largely new). The heavy quantitative Grade‑9 items are last — they're really
Grade‑10 content, so treat them as a first exposure, not mastery.

## Week 1 — Quick wins (turn the 1s into 2s)
These already scored a shaky **1** — small nudges, big morale.
- **Read/refresh:** velocity vs acceleration → NCERT04 §4.1–4.2; heat vs temperature & conduction/radiation → CK12WB ch18; echo & pitch/loudness → NCERT10 §10.6–10.7; conductor/insulator & circuit symbols → Cambridge Stage 7 §9.1–9.3; types of force & gravity → Cambridge Stage 7 §3.1.
- **Discussion:** run the tool's practice problems for g9-1/g9-2; make sure he can say each idea **in English** (his notes flagged this). Re‑score g8-5, g8-12, g8-15, g8-17, g7-14, g7-17, g7-18, g7-4, g7-6, g9-1, g9-2, g9-10.

## Week 2 — Light & optics (the big Grade‑8 gap, all 0)
- **Read:** Cambridge **Stage 8 §6.1 Reflection, §6.2 Refraction, §6.3 Making rainbows** (≈p.198–212) — the on‑level source; plus CK12WB ch22 *Visible Light* (p.265).
- **Practice:** CK12WB ch22 worksheets (answers ch26.22). Use the test's reflection/refraction/prism diagrams.
- **Discussion:** law of reflection, why light bends, prism → colours, why a red object looks red, how we see. Re‑score g8-18…g8-23.

## Week 3 — Forces, friction & turning effect (Grade 8)
- **Read:** NCERT06 §6.1–6.3 (force, balanced/unbalanced, friction); turning forces → Cambridge Stage 8 §3.4; types of force → Cambridge Stage 8 §3.1.
- **Practice:** CK12WB ch13 *Forces*; the test's moments/seesaw problem.
- **Discussion:** balanced vs unbalanced, friction (useful vs nuisance), moment = force × distance. Re‑score g7-4, g8-6, g8-7, g8-8.

## Week 4 — Pressure, buoyancy & thermal energy (Grade 8)
- **Read:** CK12WB ch15 *Fluid Forces* — 15.1 Pressure, 15.2 Buoyancy; thermal expansion → CK12WB ch18 / Cambridge Stage 8 §3.7; Cambridge Stage 8 §3.5–3.6 (pressure).
- **Practice:** CK12WB ch15 (answers ch26.15); the test's pressure problem.
- **Discussion:** p = F/A (skis/knife), pressure with depth, atmospheric pressure, why things float (upthrust vs density). Re‑score g8-9, g8-10, g8-11, g8-14, g9-16.

## Week 5 — Magnetism & electricity basics
- **Read:** magnets/fields → SIY10 ch15 *Magnetism* or CK12WB 24.1; electromagnets & current's magnetic effect → CK12WB ch25; current/charge → SIY10 ch16 + CK12WB 23.1–23.2; define current/voltage/resistance → SIY10 ch17.
- **Practice:** CK12WB ch23–25 (answers ch26.23–26.25).
- **Discussion:** magnetic field, making/strengthening an electromagnet, current as electron flow, what voltage & resistance mean. Re‑score g8-24, g8-25, g7-16, g9-17, g9-18, g9-19, g9-23.

## Week 6 — Motion graphs, Newton's laws & momentum (Grade 9)
- **Read:** NCERT04 §4.2 (speed–time graphs: gradient = acceleration, area = distance); NCERT06 §6.4–6.6 (three laws) and §6.5/6.7 (momentum & conservation).
- **Practice:** CK12WB ch14 *Newton's Laws* (answers ch26.14); the test's speed–time‑graph and F=ma problems.
- **Discussion:** read a speed–time graph, F = m·a, action–reaction, momentum = m·v. Re‑score g9-3, g9-4, g9-5, g9-6, g9-7, g9-8, g9-9.

## Week 7 — Energy, Ohm's law, waves & the atom (Grade 9) — first exposure
Heaviest week; aim for understanding, not mastery.
- **Read:** work/KE/PE/power → NCERT07 §7.1–7.5; Ohm's law, series/parallel, power → SIY10 ch17; waves (transverse/longitudinal, v=fλ) → SIY10 ch8–9; the atom → SIY10 ch4 §4.2/4.4.
- **Practice:** the test's problems for work/KE/GPE/power (g9-11…14), Ohm's law (g9-20), P=VI (g9-22), v=fλ (g9-26).
- **Discussion:** pick the ones that clicked; leave the rest for Grade 10. Re‑score whatever improved (g9-11…g9-15, g9-20, g9-21, g9-22, g9-24…g9-29).

## Week 8 — Re‑test & close gaps
- In the tool, click **+ New** → name it **"Week 8 — re‑test"**, and re‑score everything.
- Compare with the baseline (the per‑section bars + the committed `results.json`). Give a final focused session to anything still at 0/1 that matters for Grade 10.
