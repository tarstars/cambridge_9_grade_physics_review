# Diagnostic Question Sheet — read-aloud version

Companion to `checklist.md`. For each objective there are a few quick questions to ask your
son in the Week-0 diagnostic (and to re-ask in Week 8). Read one or two per item — enough to
judge **0 / 1 / 2**, not a full exam. Codes match `checklist.md` and the syllabus PDF.

> Tip: a "2" means he can both *state* the idea and *use* it (do the calculation, draw the
> diagram, give an example). If he only recites a definition, that's usually a "1".

---

## A. Forces & Energy

**7Pf.03 — Gravity**
- What is gravity? Does it act between any two objects, or only the Earth and things on it?
- If one object had more mass, would the gravitational pull be bigger or smaller?

**7Pf.01 / 7Pf.02 — Energy changes & dissipation**
- When a ball rolls to a stop, where did its movement energy go?
- What does it mean that energy "dissipates" and becomes "less useful"?

**7Pf.04 — Vacuum**
- Is there air resistance in a vacuum? Why / why not?

**8Pf.01 — Speed**
- A car travels 150 m in 10 s. What is its speed? (Ans: 15 m/s)
- Write the formula linking speed, distance and time.

**8Pf.02 — Distance–time graphs**
- On a distance–time graph, what does a steeper line mean? What does a flat (horizontal) line mean?
- Sketch the graph of something moving fast, then stopping.

**8Pf.03 — Balanced / unbalanced forces**
- If the forces on a moving object are balanced, what happens to its motion?
- What does an unbalanced force do?

**8Pf.04 — Moments**
- What is a moment (turning force), and how do you calculate it? (moment = force × distance)
- A force of 20 N acts 0.5 m from a pivot — what's the moment? (Ans: 10 N·m)

**8Pf.05 — Pressure**
- Write the formula for pressure. (pressure = force / area)
- Why does a sharp knife cut better than a blunt one? (smaller area → bigger pressure)

**8Pf.06 — Pressure in gases/liquids**
- Using particles, why does a gas push on the walls of its container?

**8Pf.07 — Diffusion**
- What is diffusion? Give an everyday example (e.g. smell spreading across a room).

**9Pf.01 — Density & floating**
- Why does a piece of wood float but a stone sinks? (relate to density vs water)
- What does it mean for an object to be "less dense than water"?

**9Pf.02 — Heat vs temperature**
- What's the difference between heat and temperature?

**9Pf.03 — Conservation of energy**
- Can energy be created or destroyed? What can happen to it instead?

**9Pf.04 — Heat dissipation**
- Which way does heat flow — hot to cold, or cold to hot? Always?

**9Pf.05 — Conduction / convection / radiation**
- Name the three ways thermal energy transfers, and give an example of each.

**9Pf.06 — Cooling by evaporation**
- Why do you feel cold when you step out of a swimming pool / when sweat dries?

## B. Light & Sound

**7Ps.01 — Sound & vacuum**
- How does sound travel through air (what do the particles do)?
- Can sound travel through a vacuum? Why not?

**7Ps.02 — Echoes**
- What causes an echo?

**8Ps.01 — Reflection**
- State the law of reflection. (angle of incidence = angle of reflection)
- Where does light go when it hits a mirror?

**8Ps.02 — Refraction**
- What happens to light when it passes from air into glass or water? Why does it bend? (speed changes)

**8Ps.03 — Dispersion**
- What do you see when white light passes through a prism? What does that tell you about white light?

**8Ps.04 — Colour**
- Why does a red apple look red? (reflects red, absorbs other colours)

**9Ps.01 — Waveforms**
- On a waveform, which feature shows loudness? Which shows pitch? (amplitude ↔ loudness, frequency ↔ pitch)
- Draw a loud high-pitched sound vs a quiet low-pitched one.

**9Ps.02 — Interference**
- Can two sound waves add up to be louder, or cancel to be quieter? Show with waveforms.

## C. Electricity & Magnetism

**7Pe.01 / 7Pe.02 — Electron flow, conductors/insulators**
- What is an electric current, in terms of electrons?
- Give an example of a conductor and of an insulator.

**7Pe.03 / 7Pe.04 — Current in series**
- Where do you put an ammeter to measure current? (in series)
- If you add more lamps to a simple series circuit, what happens to the current?

**7Pe.05 / 9Pe.04 — Circuit symbols**
- Draw the symbols for: cell, switch, lamp, ammeter, voltmeter, resistor, variable resistor.
- Draw a circuit with a cell, a switch and two lamps.

**8Pe.01 — Magnetic field**
- What is a magnetic field? Where is it around a magnet?

**8Pe.02 / 8Pe.03 — Electromagnets**
- How do you make an electromagnet?
- Name two ways to make an electromagnet stronger.

**9Pe.01 — Parallel circuits**
- In a parallel circuit, what happens to the current at a junction? (it divides)

**9Pe.02 — Measuring current & voltage**
- How do you connect a voltmeter — in series or in parallel with a component? (parallel)
- What changes if you add another cell?

**9Pe.03 — Resistance**
- Write the formula for resistance. (resistance = voltage / current)
- A component has 6 V across it and 2 A through it — what's its resistance? (Ans: 3 Ω)
- If resistance goes up, what happens to the current (same voltage)?
