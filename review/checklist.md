# Physics Diagnostic Checklist — Cambridge Lower Secondary Science (0893)

**Use:** test your son on each item, then score it:

| Score | Meaning |
|:---:|---|
| **0** | Nothing — can't do it / hasn't met it |
| **1** | More or less — partial, shaky, needs help |
| **2** | Perfect — confident and correct |

**Scope:** The Cambridge 0893 *Physics* strand is taught across Stages 7, 8 and 9 and
builds cumulatively, so a genuine "after grade 9" review covers all three. Items are
grouped by **topic area** (not by year) so related ideas sit together. Each item is tagged
with its stage — **[S9]** = the most recent (grade-9) objectives, **[S7]/[S8]** = earlier
foundations. If time is tight, prioritise **[S9]** first, then backfill [S8] and [S7] where
scores are low.

The code (e.g. `9Pf.03`) is the official Cambridge objective reference — handy if you want
to look it up in the syllabus PDF (`docs/syllabus/`) or the textbook.

## Optional: Thinking & Working Scientifically (skills, not content)

The 0893 syllabus also has a **Thinking and Working Scientifically** strand (Stage 9 codes
`9TWS…`) covering hypotheses, planning investigations, taking measurements, spotting
anomalies, and drawing conclusions. These are *skills* tested through experiments rather
than facts to recall. Worth a light check if you want, but the content items above are the
priority for a knowledge review.
