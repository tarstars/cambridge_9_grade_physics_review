# Study references — where to read each topic to master

For every weak area in the baseline (the `0` and `1` items), this lists **which file to open and
where inside it**. All sources are the free, legal books already in `docs/textbook/`.

**Reading the page numbers**
- **NCERT** pages = the page *inside that chapter's PDF* (each chapter is its own short file, ~24 pp).
- **CK12WB** pages = the workbook's own printed page numbers (as in its Contents). In a PDF viewer that's
  about **+7** (e.g. lesson 15.1 "p.179" ≈ PDF p.186) — or just jump by the **lesson number** (the safest
  anchor). The matching **answer key** is in its Chapter 26.
- **SIY10** pages = the book's printed page (as in its Contents). In the PDF viewer that's about **+16**
  (e.g. "ch17, book p.280" ≈ PDF p.296). Easiest: open the PDF bookmarks/Contents and jump by chapter title.
- CK‑12 is **middle-school level** (closest to Grade 7–8); NCERT is solid Grade‑9 mechanics; SIY10 is a
  notch higher (good for waves, electricity, the atom). All are in English — useful for the terminology gap.

**Suggested order** (from the diagnostic): quick wins (the `1`s) → Grade‑8 light/optics → Grade‑8
forces & pressure → Grade‑9 mechanics → Grade‑9 electricity & waves.

## Note on the "English terminology" gap
Your notes flagged needing the **English terms / standard symbols** (g7-4, g7-18, g8-4, g8-17). Because all
the sources above are in English, reading the relevant short section first — then doing the test question —
builds that vocabulary directly. The circuit‑symbol diagram in the test (g7-18) shows the standard symbols.
